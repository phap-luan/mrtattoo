Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a tattoo cost in Hanoi?

Mini tattoos at Mr Tattoo Hanoi Studio start from $10. Medium and large pieces are quoted by size, detail, color and placement. Message us on WhatsApp for a free quote.

Where is Mr Tattoo Hanoi Studio?

We are on the 2nd floor of 106 Hàng Buồm, in the Hanoi Old Quarter (Hoàn Kiếm), and open 24 hours, every day.

Do tattoos hurt?

Pain levels vary depending on the placement and individual pain threshold. We will advise you on suitable placements.

Are your equipment and needles sterile?

100% single-use sterile needles and disposable equipment opened right in front of you.

How do I care for my new tattoo?

Keep it clean, apply ointment as instructed, avoid direct sunlight and swimming for the first 2 weeks.
