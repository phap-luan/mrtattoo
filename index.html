Frequently Asked Questions

Do tattoos hurt?

Pain levels vary depending on the placement and individual pain threshold. We will advise you on suitable placements.

How are tattoo prices calculated?

Prices depend on size, detail, color, and placement. Send us your idea for a free quote.

How do I care for my new tattoo?

Keep it clean, apply ointment as instructed, avoid direct sunlight and swimming for the first 2 weeks.

Are your equipment and needles sterile?

100% single-use sterile needles and disposable equipment opened right in front of you.
