About Mr Tattoo Hanoi

From one small room in the Old Quarter to a studio travelers and locals trust with their stories.

How It Began

"We started with one chair, one lamp, and a simple promise: clean, careful tattoos at honest prices."

Mr Tattoo Hanoi Studio opened in 2012 on the second floor of a narrow tube house at 106 Hàng Buồm, in the middle of Hanoi's Old Quarter. [Founder Name] had been tattooing for [X] years and wanted a place where first-timers felt as welcome as collectors.

In the early days, most clients were friends and neighbors from the surrounding streets. Word spread, then backpackers and travelers started climbing our stairs, asking for a small piece to remember Vietnam by. Many of them came back with friends.

Today we are a small team of artists working in styles from fine line and blackwork to Vietnamese motifs and cover-ups. Every design is drawn for the person wearing it, and every needle is single-use and opened in front of you.

The Old Quarter is still our home. Come up the stairs, say hello, and tell us your story.
