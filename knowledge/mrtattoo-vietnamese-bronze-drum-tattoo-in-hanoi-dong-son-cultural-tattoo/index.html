VIETNAMESE BRONZE DRUM TATTOO

Dating back thousands of years, the Dong Son bronze drum represents the history, traditions and creativity of ancient Vietnamese people. Its distinctive patterns feature a radiant sun at the center, concentric circles, flying birds, traditional boats and scenes of ancient Vietnamese life.

One of its most recognizable motifs is the Lac Bird (Chim Lạc), a powerful symbol associated with ancient Vietnamese culture.

At MR.TATTOO, a professional tattoo studio in Hanoi Old Quarter, we transform these traditional Vietnamese patterns into unique and meaningful tattoos.

Whether you are looking for a detailed Dong Son bronze drum tattoo, a traditional Vietnamese cultural tattoo, a minimalist fine line design or a small travel souvenir tattoo, our artists can help bring your idea to life.

A Vietnamese bronze drum tattoo is more than body art. It is a beautiful way to remember your journey through Vietnam and carry a little piece of its history with you forever.
