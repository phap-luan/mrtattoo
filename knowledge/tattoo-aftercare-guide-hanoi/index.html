Complete Tattoo Aftercare Guide: Healing Tips for Travelers in Hanoi

"Everything you need to know about caring for your fresh tattoo in humid tropical weather, from washing techniques to ointment application."

Getting a tattoo while traveling in Vietnam is an unforgettable experience. However, caring for fresh ink in Hanoi's climate requires specific attention to hygiene and daily routines.

1. The First 24 Hours

Leave the protective bandage on for 2 to 4 hours. Gently wash the area with lukewarm water and antibacterial fragrance-free soap.

2. Keep It Clean and Moisturized

Apply a thin layer of recommended aftercare balm 2-3 times daily. Avoid soaking in bathtubs or swimming pools for at least 2 weeks.
