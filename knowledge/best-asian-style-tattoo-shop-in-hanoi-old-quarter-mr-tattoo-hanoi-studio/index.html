​The Art of Asian & Vietnamese Traditional Tattoos

​Asian tattooing is more than just ink on skin—it is a storytelling tradition built on balance, movement, and deep cultural meaning. Unlike generic flash designs, an authentic Oriental piece is drawn to flow naturally with the anatomy of your body.

​At Mr. Tattoo Hanoi Studio, our artists specialize in a wide range of Asian tattoo traditions:

​Vietnamese Cultural & Heritage Tattoos: Dong Son bronze drum patterns, lotus flowers, traditional bamboo, water buffaloes, and iconic Hanoi landmarks.

​Oriental Mythical Creatures: Eastern dragons, phoenixes, Koi fish, Foo Dogs (guardian lions), and cranes—each representing strength, resilience, prosperity, or harmony.

​Asian Brushwork & Sumi-e Style: Expressive ink-wash strokes inspired by classical East Asian calligraphy and landscape painting.

​Micro & Fine Line Asian Art: Delicate, modern interpretations of Eastern symbols—perfect for travelers wanting a subtle yet meaningful souvenir.

​Custom Full-Sleeve & Back Pieces: Large-scale traditional compositions engineered with dynamic wind bars, water waves, and clouds.
